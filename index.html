Everything you need to know about privacy, pricing, and how our tools work.

Yes, 100% safe. Unlike Chrome extensions that hook into your live seller panel, SmallSellerSupport is a standalone web toolkit. We never ask for your Meesho, Flipkart, or Amazon password, OTP, or API keys.

Completely private. Our Bulk Label Cropper & SKU Sorter and all margin calculators run 100% inside your own browser. Your customer addresses, PDFs, and pricing data are never uploaded to or stored on any external server.

Both! Every free and Pro tool works directly in Chrome, Safari, or any mobile browser—no app download or software installation required.

Yes! You can crop Meesho, Flipkart, and Amazon shipping labels to 4×6 thermal size (or 4-per-A4), sort them by SKU, and generate a Page 1 Picking List for free—with zero annoying pop-up ads.

Activation is instant. As soon as you enter your 12-digit UPI UTR number and WhatsApp number in the unlock popup, all Pro tools open immediately while our team verifies the UTR in the background.
