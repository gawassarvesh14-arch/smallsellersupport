Refund & Cancellation Policy

Legal Entity: SmallSellerSupport (Proprietorship) • Udyam Reg. No: UDYAM-GA-01-0066334

At SmallSellerSupport (https://smallsellersupport.com), we aim to ensure complete transparency regarding our digital SaaS subscriptions.

1. Subscription Cancellation Policy

  • You may cancel your Pro subscription at any time before your next billing renewal date by sending a cancellation request to support@smallsellersupport.com from your registered email address.
  • Upon cancellation, you will not be charged for subsequent billing cycles, and your Pro access will remain active until the end of your current paid period.

2. Refund Policy

  • Because SmallSellerSupport provides instant digital access to software tools and calculators, subscription fees are generally non-refundable once Pro access is activated.
  • Exceptions (100% Refund Eligible): You are eligible for a full refund if:
    • You were charged twice due to a payment gateway or technical error.
    • A verified technical defect on our platform prevents you from accessing Pro features and our support team cannot resolve it within 48 hours.
    • You request a refund within 48 hours of your initial purchase without substantial usage of Pro tools.

3. Refund Processing Timeline

Once a refund request is reviewed and approved, the amount will be credited back to your original payment method (UPI, Debit/Credit Card, or Netbanking) within 5 to 7 business days.

4. How to Request a Refund or Cancellation

Email your payment transaction ID and registered email/phone number to support@smallsellersupport.com. Our support team responds within 24 business hours.
